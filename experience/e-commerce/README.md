# E-commerce & International Sourcing

## Independent E-commerce Activity

For approximately one year, I independently managed a small-scale e-commerce activity based on international product sourcing.

The activity involved the complete process from supplier negotiation and purchasing to international shipping, stock management, and local sales.

## Responsibilities

- Sourced products from Alibaba
- Communicated and negotiated with Chinese suppliers in **English**
- Negotiated product prices and order terms
- Placed and followed international orders
- Coordinated shipment from China to Burkina Faso through freight-forwarding / transit services
- Followed the logistics process until goods were received
- Managed inventory and stock levels
- Set selling prices and handled local sales

## Skills Demonstrated

**International sourcing · Supplier negotiation · English business communication · Purchasing · International logistics · Inventory management · Pricing · Sales · Independent business management**
