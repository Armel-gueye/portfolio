# Automated Lead Collection & Enrichment

## Business Lead Collection and Qualification Pipeline

This n8n workflow automates the collection and enrichment of business leads.

## What It Demonstrates

- Telegram-triggered execution
- Business discovery through Google Maps data
- Website crawling and scraping
- Contact-data extraction
- Email, phone, and social-profile extraction
- AI-assisted lead analysis and qualification
- Phone validation and normalization
- Service classification
- Conditional routing
- Batch and loop processing
- PostgreSQL / Supabase storage
- Upsert and conflict handling
- Error notifications

## Main Integrations

n8n, Apify, Google Maps data, Crawl4AI, Cohere, PostgreSQL, Supabase, and Telegram.
